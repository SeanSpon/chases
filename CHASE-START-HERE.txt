AUTOCART FOR CHASE — VERSION 0.2.0

DOWNLOAD: https://github.com/SeanSpon/chases/releases/latest
Under Assets, download AutoCart-Setup-0.2.0.exe and run it.
Requires Windows x64 and Chrome or Edge. Installer is unsigned.
Private repo: Sean can download the installer and send it to you.

1. Open AutoCart and click HOW TO USE THIS at the top.
2. Paste all your product links, one per line. Click Add links.
3. Set your maximum item price, total budget and total number of items.
4. Watch-only spends NOTHING. Leave Auto-buy off if you only want alerts.
5. For real orders, click Set up auto-buy beside EACH product.
6. Open the setup browser. Sign into the store and save your address/payment
   DIRECTLY WITH THE STORE. AutoCart does not copy your normal browser's cards.
7. Follow the Pick buttons to teach the product and checkout steps.
   Picking does not activate a store button. NEVER click Place order yourself
   during setup. Full step-by-step directions are in the app.
8. Save setup, then Test without buying. The test never clicks the final order
   button but DOES add to your real cart. EMPTY THE TEST CART afterward.
9. Turn on Auto-buy for that product and accept the real-purchase prompt.
10. Press Start. Closing the window keeps the app in the system tray.

Auto-buy can CHARGE THE PAYMENT METHOD SAVED IN YOUR STORE ACCOUNT.
One unit per link, once. Exact one-item cart and selected final USD total
must pass the checks. Correct final-total selection is essential.
Not all stores work. CAPTCHA, CVV, login or bank approval need you.
No real retailer has been certified without its links and account setup.

Keep your computer awake and connected. Right-click the tray icon to stop/quit.
STOP cannot undo an order already submitted.
In history, UNCERTAIN/SUBMITTING means it may have charged. Check the store.
The app will not automatically retry those orders, even after restart.
