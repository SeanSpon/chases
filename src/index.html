How to use AutoCart

  1. Paste your links. Add multiple stores or products at once.
  2. Set your limits. Save the maximum item price, total budget and item count.
  3. Just want alerts? Leave Auto-buy off. Press Start. This spends nothing.
  4. Want automatic orders? Choose Set up auto-buy beside a product. It needs one setup and a checkout test for EACH product. This is not a universal retailer integration.
  5. Sign in directly with the store. The setup browser has its own local profile. Save your shipping address and payment method in your STORE ACCOUNT. AutoCart does not copy cards from your normal Chrome/Edge profile or store card numbers. Some stores always require CVV or a bank approval; those cannot complete unattended.
  6. Teach the checkout buttons. Use each Pick button, switch to the store browser, and click the highlighted element. Picking does not activate it. Click the actual store button again yourself to move to the next page. Never manually click the final order button during setup.
  7. Test without buying. AutoCart replays to the final checkout screen without clicking Place order. This CAN add to your real cart. Empty the cart afterward. If a test fails, fix setup or leave watch-only on.
  8. Turn on Auto-buy. This authorizes real orders for that product with the saved store payment method, within your limits. Then press Start. Each link buys ONE item ONCE. The cart must contain only that item.
  9. Leave the computer awake. Closing the window keeps it running in the tray. Right-click the tray icon to stop or quit.
  10. Check order history. Purchased means a receipt was detected. Uncertain/submitting means it may have charged: check the retailer. AutoCart will NOT retry those orders, even after a restart.

CAPTCHA, login prompts, bank approval and anti-bot blocks require your attention. AutoCart does not bypass them. Generic selectors can stop working after a store redesign. Real stores have not been certified or tested without your links.
